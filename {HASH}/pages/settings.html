Oa10712's Pocket TCG Tracker

Select an expansion from the list above to get started. Once you have an expansion open, you will see a list of all cards in that expansion on the left, a pack overview on the top right, and a mission overview on the bottom right. Select the checkboxes for cards you have and the pack overview and missions will update accordingly.

The pack overview will display how many cards you are missing from each, as well as the % chance of getting a new card from each pack.

The mission overview contains a list of all expansion missions. Select one to view the card requirements. Once you have a mission opened, you can click the "Show missing requirements" button to filter the card list and pack overview to only show missing cards from the selected mission. You can also click any requirement name to filter for that specific requirement only.

Want to suggest a feature or report a bug? Check the Issues tab below.
